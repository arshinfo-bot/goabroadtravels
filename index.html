Residence Permit Support – Portugal, Italy & Other European Countries

We can help collect documents, explain official application steps and assist with administrative preparation for a possible application to the German Ausländerbehörde. A residence permit issued by Portugal or Italy does not automatically permit employment or long-term residence in Germany, and it cannot simply be exchanged for a German permit. Eligibility depends on nationality, residence status, job offer and German immigration law. EU long-term resident status may provide a different legal route, subject to conditions.

Administrative handling fee: €1,500 including VAT for an agreed eligible case involving a qualifying permanent or EU long-term residence status from another European country, after individual eligibility and scope review. This is our service fee only, not a government fee or payment for a residence permit. Government fees, translations and other third-party costs may be additional. No approval or job placement is guaranteed.

Temporary / short-term residence permits: Please enquire first about eligibility, required documents and an individual price quotation. Not all applicants qualify for a German residence permit through this route.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before you apply?

Does the €110 package include the embassy visa fee?

No. €110 is the administrative support fee per applicant. Government fees, appointments and third-party costs are separate.

Can you guarantee a visa approval?

No. Only the responsible immigration authority or consulate decides whether a visa is granted.

Can I instantly download a flight or hotel booking?

Not currently. We provide an enquiry and coordination service; genuine bookings must be confirmed by the relevant provider.

Do I need a flight and hotel reservation for a UK visitor visa?

Not necessarily. The required evidence depends on the destination and application. We follow official guidance and do not recommend unnecessary reservations.

Can you help with a business invitation or trade fair?

Yes. We can help prepare invitation requests and identify official event registration. Only the real host or event organizer can issue genuine confirmations.
